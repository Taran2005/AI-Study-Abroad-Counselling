--- TEST SCORES & ENGLISH PROFICIENCY ---

IELTS (International English Language Testing System):
- Academic version required for university admissions
- Scored on a 9-band scale (Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking)
- Most UK/Ireland universities require 6.0–7.5 overall with minimum 5.5–6.5 per band
- Test fee: Approx. ₹16,250 / $232
- Validity: 2 years from test date
- Tip: Start preparation 3–6 months before the target test date

PTE Academic (Pearson Test of English):
- Computer-based test, results in 2–5 business days
- Scored 10–90
- Most universities accept PTE scores of 50–76 (equivalent to IELTS 6.0–7.0)
- Test fee: Approx. ₹15,900 / $210
- Validity: 2 years

TOEFL iBT:
- Internet-based test by ETS
- Scored 0–120
- Most UK/Ireland universities accept 80–100+
- Test fee: Approx. ₹16,900 / $205
- Validity: 2 years

Duolingo English Test:
- Online test, results in 48 hours
- Scored 10–160
- Accepted by some UK/Ireland universities (check specific requirements)
- Test fee: Approx. $59
- Validity: 2 years

--- VISA INFORMATION ---

UK Student Visa (Tier 4 / Student Route):
- Required for courses longer than 6 months
- Must have a Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS) from a licensed sponsor
- Financial requirements: Proof of funds to cover tuition + living costs (£1,334/month for London, £1,023/month outside London) for up to 9 months
- Application fee: £490
- Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS): £776/year
- Processing time: 3–6 weeks
- Can work up to 20 hours/week during term, full-time during holidays
- Post-study work: Graduate Route visa allows 2 years (3 years for PhD) of unrestricted work after graduation

Ireland Student Visa (Stamp 2):
- Required for non-EEA students for courses longer than 3 months
- Must have acceptance letter from recognised Irish institution
- Financial requirements: Proof of €10,000 in your bank account
- Application fee: €60 (single entry) / €100 (multi-entry)
- Processing time: 4–8 weeks
- Can work up to 20 hours/week during term, 40 hours/week during holidays
- Post-study work: Third Level Graduate Programme allows 1–2 years stay to find employment

--- SCHOLARSHIPS & FINANCIAL AID ---

UK Scholarships:
- Chevening Scholarships (UK Government, fully funded Master's for outstanding professionals)
- Commonwealth Scholarships (for students from Commonwealth countries)
- GREAT Scholarships (for students from select countries, £10,000 towards tuition)
- University-specific merit scholarships (£2,000–£20,000)

Ireland Scholarships:
- Government of Ireland International Education Scholarships (€10,000 per year for Master's/PhD)
- Science Foundation Ireland scholarships
- University-specific scholarships (€2,000–€10,000)

Tips for Scholarship Applications:
1. Apply early — many deadlines are 6–9 months before course start
2. Prepare a strong personal statement
3. Showcase academic achievements AND extracurricular activities
4. Get strong recommendation letters
5. Research university-specific scholarships — many go unclaimed

--- APPLICATION TIMELINE ---

For September Intake (Main Intake):
- 12–18 months before: Research universities and courses
- 9–12 months before: Take IELTS/PTE/TOEFL
- 6–9 months before: Submit applications through UCAS (UK) or directly (Ireland)
- 4–6 months before: Receive offers, pay deposits
- 2–4 months before: Apply for student visa
- 1–2 months before: Arrange accommodation, book flights
- Pre-departure: Attend orientation sessions

For January Intake (Limited courses):
- Shift timeline forward by 4 months
- Fewer courses available, but less competition

Types available:
1. University Halls of Residence (recommended for first-year students)
2. Private student accommodation (managed properties like Unite Students, IQ Student)
3. Shared flats/houses (cheaper, more independence)

--- FAQ ---

Q: Can I work while studying in the UK?
A: Yes, Tier 4 student visa allows 20 hours/week part-time during term and full-time during holidays.

Q: Is a gap year accepted by UK/Ireland universities?
A: Yes, most universities accept gap years. Some may ask for an explanation in your personal statement.

Q: What is UCAS?
A: UCAS (Universities and Colleges Admissions Service) is the centralised application system for UK undergraduate courses. You can apply to up to 5 universities through one application.

Q: Do I need to take IELTS if I studied in English-medium school?
A: Most UK/Ireland universities still require an English test unless your previous degree was from an English-speaking country (UK, USA, Australia, Canada, etc.). Some accept a school waiver — check individual university policies.

Q: What is the Graduate Route visa?
A: The Graduate Route visa (UK) allows international students to stay and work in the UK for 2 years after completing their degree (3 years for PhD). No sponsorship needed.

Q: Can I bring my family on a student visa?
A: UK: Only if you're on a course of 9+ months at postgraduate level and have sufficient funds. Ireland: Generally not for student visa holders.

Q: How much bank balance do I need to show?
A: UK: Tuition fees + £1,334/month (London) or £1,023/month (outside London) for up to 9 months. Ireland: Minimum €10,000.
